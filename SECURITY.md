# Security

Please report a vulnerability in Mailway privately, not in a public issue.

- Email [security@mailway.net](mailto:security@mailway.net), or use [the contact form](https://mailway.net/contact?topic=security).
- Include what you found, where, and the steps to reproduce it.

Mailway's responsible disclosure terms (how fast we confirm receipt, how we keep you informed, credit, and our promise not to take legal action over good-faith research) are on the [Trust page](https://mailway.net/trust#disclosure). Both contacts are also published in [`security.txt`](https://mailway.net/.well-known/security.txt).
